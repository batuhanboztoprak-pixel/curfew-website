Privacy

Curfew helps with something personal, so privacy is built into how it works — starting with the apps you choose to block.

Your app choices stay on your iPhone

Curfew blocks apps using Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls). You pick apps with Apple's own picker, and the system gives Curfew only opaque tokens — Curfew never learns which apps you chose.
