A pause, exactly when you need one

No willpower lectures, no guilt. You decide ahead of time which hours are risky and how firm Curfew should be — then it holds the line for you.

Friction mode

Opening a monitored app during risk hours brings up a pause screen first. You can still get through — tap Continue anyway, wait a few seconds, and tap again — but that small gap is often all it takes for the impulse to pass.

Hard Block mode

For the nights you know you'll bargain with yourself. Monitored apps are fully blocked until your risk hours end, with no override.

Streak tracking

Curfew counts the nights in a row you made it through your risk hours without opening a monitored app, and keeps your best streak alongside it.

Private by design

Blocking runs on your iPhone through Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls). You choose apps with Apple's own picker, which keeps your selection private to your device — Curfew never sees which apps you picked.

Set it up once

  1. Pick your apps

    Choose the apps that tend to win late at night — delivery apps, or anything else.

  2. Set your risk hours

    Tell Curfew when you're most vulnerable, like 10 PM to 4 AM.

  3. Choose how firm

    A gentle pause with Friction, or a full stop with Hard Block. Change it any time.
